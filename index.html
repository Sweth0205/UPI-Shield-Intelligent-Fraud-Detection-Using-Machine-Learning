💡 Understanding the Fields

Account Age: The number of days since the user's account was created. Newer accounts (less than 90 days) are considered higher risk as fraudsters often create new accounts for fraudulent activities.
Chargebacks: When a customer disputes a transaction and requests a refund, it's called a chargeback. A history of multiple chargebacks indicates suspicious activity and increases fraud risk.
